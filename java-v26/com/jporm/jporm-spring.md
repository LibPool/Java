## 获取地址

- Maven 仓库地址：https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/com/jporm/jporm-spring/
- Maven 坐标：`com.jporm:jporm-spring`
- pom.xml 引用：`<dependency><groupId>com.jporm</groupId><artifactId>jporm-spring</artifactId><version>8.0.1</version></dependency>`
